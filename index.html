🏆 Crew Board

📊 Manager view: shift scores (Kristen only)

How the score works: each shift gets a score where 1.00 = met expectations. Expected output is about 1 task per person per hour, softened when the store is busy (when the store is busy the target drops, so customers come first), and scaled to staffing: two people on shift are held to a two-person output, one person to a one-person output. A shift's score blends how the whole crew did against its target with how you did against yours. Your leaderboard number is the median of your shift scores, so one shift a week competes fairly with every shift, and one weird shift can't swing it. Improving on your own last week earns 📈. Credit goes to whoever's code was entered, approved tasks only.
